# Post 2 / Where we started from

Before the experiment, the baseline. In 2023 we bought an automated luggage
storage business in Venice from an owner who ran the physical side fine and had
no idea how to sell it online. Nothing was broken. The business was invisible
and manual. We applied the bare minimum of digital knowledge across three areas.
This is what we found, what we changed and what it produced. The AI experiment
only means something measured against these numbers.

**THE OBJECTIVE**

- An extra source of income that does not require our presence.
- An automated luggage storage should already be exactly that: machines, a door, a code, no staff.
- It was not. That gap was the whole opportunity.

**WHAT WE FOUND**

- Access and usage instructions written badly. Customers arrived and did not know what to do.
- So the previous owner had to be reachable all day to explain it. A business designed to run without people needed a person permanently on call.
- No online booking. Internal operations run by hand.
- The phone ringing constantly, always for the same predictable problems.

**WHAT WE CHANGED**

Three areas. None of them sophisticated.

1. User experience. Rewrote from scratch how you find the place, how you get in, how you use it.
2. Automation. Online booking and internal operations first. Then we mapped every possible customer problem, one by one, and automated or prevented most of them. Target: zero inbound calls.
3. Online selling. Basic level. We are not experts and we did not pretend to be.

**RESULT**

- Revenue doubled.
- Costs went down first, then back above the starting point. Almost all of that increase is ad spend, with positive ROAS.
- Neither of us lives in Venice any more. The business runs there without us.

**THE POINT**

We applied nothing advanced. The bare minimum was enough to double it.
That says more about the sector than about us.
