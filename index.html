Case Study 01

Finding the data gap that was quietly skewing pickup complaints

DHL Express · Customer Experience Regional Manager, EU · Ongoing, end-to-end owner

Context

I own pickup customer experience end to end across DHL Express's European region, coordinating regional, global, and local stakeholders to diagnose issues, pilot fixes, and scale them across the network.

Challenge

Pickup-related complaints were rising and nobody could see why. Two problems were tangled together: customers were offered pickup time windows that did not match real operational capacity, and courier no-shows were being logged the same way as genuine customer unavailability, because no data category existed to separate them. That second gap had corrupted root-cause data for years, worse on ad-hoc pickups than scheduled ones.

What I built and piloted

  • Driver verification app: requires the courier to photograph the doorbell and captures geolocation when marking a pickup as "customer not ready." Ends false attribution at the source.
  • Capacity realignment: realigned the pickup windows offered to customers with real operational capacity.
  • Proactive failed-pickup automation: automatically messages the customer when a pickup fails and supports rescheduling, something the process never did before.
−20% overall company complaints in the Austria/Germany driver-verification pilot
+10-point lift in pickup NPS in the Hungary capacity-realignment pilot
Zero detractors so far on the topic in the Portugal/Sweden proactive-messaging pilot

Status: all three are live pilots; scale-up across the network is in progress.

Case Study 02

Cutting a multi-week pricing cycle without waiting for other teams' systems

DHL Express · Senior Continuous Improvement Internal Consultant, EU · 2023

Context

The regional tender and contract-pricing process sets pricing for DHL Express's major-account contracts across the EU, including global and corporate accounts with EU lanes, covering the bulk of the division's key-account revenue.

Challenge

The cycle took 33 days. Part of the delay sat with other departments, especially Pricing, whose systems were locked into brittle Excel macros and SharePoints they were unwilling to change. There was no clean API to build against.

What I built

A production tool combining Power Apps and Power Automate, with heavy LLM support during the build, engineered around the legacy systems rather than waiting for them to modernize.

33 → 23 days (about 30% faster) on the regional tender cycle, across the bulk of the EU division's key-account revenue
Case Study 03

Automating a small business until it runs itself

Nutrie Srl · Owner · Venice, Italy · 2023 to present

Context

Nutrie is a self-service luggage-storage business in Venice, acquired with a partner in 2023 and run entirely remotely.

Challenge

Bookings and customer questions were handled manually, generating 4 to 5 enquiries a day, all of it my time.

What I built

I automated the booking flow and customer communication myself, so the business runs without day-to-day intervention.

Under 1 enquiry/week, down from 4 to 5 a day
Revenue roughly doubled (about €23k to €47k) over two years, with costs held flat
Runs hands-off for weeks at a time; about 2 hours a month of admin
