## What it is

The core product of [[self-identita-Nutrie]]: automated lockers for luggage storage, paid by time (hours or days), self-service access with no staff, through a kiosk taking cash, card and contactless. The site is accessible **during opening hours** (Venice 6:30-23:30, Pisa 7:30-22:30, [[listino-prezzi-2026]]): it is *not* 24 hour access, wording corrected on 28/08/2026. Video surveillance, on the other hand, is continuous. Run through the software described in [[reparto-gestionale-locker]].

Hardware inherited with the acquisition of the business unit (14/03/2023): about 15 lockers (2 minibox24 plus 13 laminated safe 3 special), 1 electronic kiosk, 1 coin box, 1 coin changer, video surveillance (5 cameras). *Source: the schedule of depreciable assets attached to the transfer deed.*

## Sales channels

Revenue from this product comes from three channels, each tracked separately in the financial sheets. See [[concetto-onsite]] (paying on site) and [[concetto-marketplace]] (resale through third party platforms). The third channel, "Booking" (direct online reservation via PayPal, Nexi or bank transfer), has no concept note of its own, a deliberate scope decision.

## Prices

The public price list is in [[listino-prezzi-2026]] (read off the website on 03/08/2026): in Venice a single locker size on hourly bands (4,50 € up to 2 h, rising to 20 € for the day), in Pisa two sizes, Small and Big, with the Big aligned to the Venice prices. The contractual rules that go with the list (late penalties, maximum duration, abandoned luggage) are in [[doc-condizioni-di-utilizzo]].

## Dimensions and capacity

Venice lockers: **45 x 60 x 80 cm**, capacity stated in the FAQ as "four hand luggage bags or two large bags". Maximum weight allowed per bag: **25 kg**. Access through a **6 digit PIN** chosen by the customer at booking or at the kiosk. *Source: the FAQ and terms of use on the website, 03/08/2026.*

Pisa lockers, two sizes: **Small 40 x 45 x 60 cm**, **Big 90 x 40 x 60 cm**. *Stated by Giacomo on 18/08/2026.* They are not published on the website and the order of the axes was not specified.

The **Pisa Big is 216 litres, exactly like the single Venice locker** (45 x 60 x 80 = 216 l), despite a different shape: wider and lower. The Small is half that, 108 litres. That is the fact that makes the price alignment between the Pisa Big and the Venice locker defensible, as already recorded in [[listino-prezzi-2026]]: same volume, same price. In words, the "Small" is stated as fitting at most two hand luggage bags and not suitable for large ones; the "Big" for several hand luggage bags or large ones. How the 18 lockers split between the two sizes is in [[listino-prezzi-2026]].
