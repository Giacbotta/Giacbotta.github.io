## Definition

A seasonal pattern observed in the Booking channel (direct online reservations) of [[prodotto-deposito-self-service]]: the month with the highest number of customers is **October**, in both years for which there is a verified, reliable Booking figure:

- 2024: October = 71 customers (the highest month of the year), see [[kpi-2024]]
- 2025: October = 51 customers (the highest month of the year), see [[kpi-2025]]

Source: [[strumento-performance-nutrie]], the "Clienti - Booking" rows of the "2025 - Report" and "2026 - Report" sheets.

## Why Booking only

The Onsite channel was not used for this analysis because it is documented as unreliable (values that do not line up between sheets) in [[strumento-performance-nutrie]]. Booking is the only channel verified as identical, month by month, across the different versions of the file.

## GAP

Only two complete years available (2024, 2025): it is not possible to confirm whether the October peak is a stable pattern over more than two cycles, and the cause (events, high tourist season) is not documented in the raw material.
